Apple Intelligence is Apple's built-in system for processing language on your Mac. EnviousWispr can use it to tidy up your dictation by removing filler words and fixing punctuation. It is free, needs no API key, and keeps your text on your Mac. A fresh install starts with it selected, but it only works on macOS 26 or later.

### What do I need for Apple Intelligence polish?

- **macOS version.** You need macOS 26 or later.
- **Hardware.** You need a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence.
- **Model availability.** The on-device model must be available.

### How do I turn on Apple Intelligence polish?

1. **Check availability in macOS.** On macOS 26, open **System Settings** > **Apple Intelligence & Siri** and switch it on. On macOS 27 or later, use the status on EnviousWispr's **AI Polish** page. If macOS reports that Apple Intelligence was not enabled, the cause may be unknown and System Settings may not offer a fix. You can keep dictating or choose another model.
2. **Open AI Polish.** In EnviousWispr, open **Settings** > **AI Polish**.
3. **Select Apple Intelligence.** Choose **Apple Intelligence** under **On this Mac**.

You know it worked when your next dictation comes back with the filler words removed and the punctuation corrected.

### What if Apple Intelligence is not available?

EnviousWispr shows what macOS reported at the last check on the **AI Polish** page. For some statuses, Apple does not say the underlying cause. Read [_Apple Intelligence Not Available_](/help/apple-intelligence-not-available/).

### Which on-device option should I use on an older Mac?

If your Mac cannot run macOS 26, pick **EG-1** or **S1-mini** under **On this Mac**. Both polish on your Mac without Apple Intelligence, and neither needs a key. EG-1 is our own model and is marked **Recommended**. S1-mini by Superwhisper is smaller.
