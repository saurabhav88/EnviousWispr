On Android you start a dictation from the floating bubble that appears beside the keyboard. You can tap it and tap again to finish, or hold it while you talk and let go to finish.

### Tap or hold the bubble

The bubble appears whenever a text box is active, just above the keyboard on the right edge unless you moved it.

| How you start | How you finish | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Tap** the bubble | Tap the **check** | Longer thoughts, hands free while you talk |
| **Hold** the bubble | Let go | Short messages |

When you tap, the bubble opens into a recording pill with a clock, a sound meter, an **X** to cancel and a **check** to finish. When you hold, the pill shows **Recording. Let go to finish.** A recording stops at 10 minutes, with a warning when less than a minute is left.

### Move or hide the bubble

**Move it.** Drag the bubble. It snaps to the nearer edge of the screen and stays there. Dragging never starts a recording.

**Hide it.** Drag the bubble onto **Drop to hide**. It stays hidden until the next text box you open.

**Change its look.** Open Settings, then **Appearance** > **Floating button**, and pick **Smoke**, **Clear** or **Bare**.

### Other ways to start

**Quick Settings tile.** Add the EnviousWispr tile to your Quick Settings panel. Tap it to start and tap it again to stop. Because the tile is not tied to a text box, your words go to the clipboard, ready to paste.

**The microphone button.** Inside the app, tap the microphone button at the top of the screen.

While a dictation runs, the notification shows **EnviousWispr is listening** with **Stop** and **Cancel**, if you allowed notifications.

### How you know it worked

Your finished words appear in the text box after the pill closes. If they do not, see [Text Did Not Appear on Android](/help/android-text-did-not-land/).
