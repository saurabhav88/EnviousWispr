History holds your saved dictations, the files you transcribed and the meetings you recorded. We rebuilt it in EnviousWispr 2.6.1 so it feels natural to use. Find what you said, see what happened to it, and use it again without hunting.

## A tab for each kind of recording

History now has separate tabs for **Dictations**, **Transcripts** and **Meetings**. Each tab keeps its own search and filters. Filter by date, the app you dictated into, or the call app a meeting came from.

## Hear what you said

Turn on **Save dictation recordings**, and saved dictations keep a recording on your Mac. Press play on a card that has one, or drag along the wave to jump to a moment. Play it to settle "did I really say that?", or to hear how you said a name that came out wrong.

Recordings stay on your Mac, and you choose how long to keep them in **Settings** > **App Settings** > **Recordings**. Keep one for good with **Keep this recording**, or delete the audio and keep the text with **Delete recording, keep text**.

## See what polishing changed

AI polishing helps, though it is fair to want to know what it did. When polishing changes a dictation, click **Show edits** to switch between three views: **Polished**, **Marked up** and **Original**. In the marked-up view, click any marked word to compare the text before and after AI Polish, and, when **Hear it** appears, play that moment.

If you prefer the text before AI Polish, **Restore original text** puts it back in History. Change your mind, and **Use polished text** brings the polished version back.

## Polish again, then pick the better version

Sometimes you want a second opinion from another model. Choose **Polish again with** and pick any model you have set up. EnviousWispr places the current text and the new text side by side so you can keep whichever reads better. The same works for file transcripts, and for meeting transcripts with **Polish transcript with**.

## Transcripts you can listen along to

Each file you transcribed gets its own card and page. When the original file plays, you can listen at speeds from 0.75× to 2×, and for transcripts with saved times, **Follow playback** keeps the spoken line in view. Rename a transcript, or transcribe it again with another speech engine, without touching the original file.

## Meetings, notes and summaries in one place

Open a meeting to find your notes and the AI summary beside the transcript and recording. While a meeting records, **Mark moment** adds a timed marker and **Action item** switches the current note between a task and ordinary text. The first summary saves when it is ready. When you rewrite one, EnviousWispr shows both versions so you can keep the better of the two.

## Still private

None of this changes where your History lives. Your dictations, transcripts, meetings and recordings are stored on your Mac, and Envious Labs never receives a copy. If you choose cloud polish, the text, cleanup instructions and any custom words it needs go to the provider you picked. Cloud summaries send the meeting transcript, notes and meeting details, including calendar invitees when available.

Find the full guide to every button on the [History help page](/help/transcript-history/). If you have not tried EnviousWispr yet, it is a free, open-source dictation app for Mac, and you can [download it here](https://enviouswispr.com/).
