## Q1. Direction

The core direction is right: use the system dictionary for vocabulary, use context to protect names, refuse learned words, and fail toward keeping the capital.

Three revisions are required:

- Refusing nouns is defensible only as a permanent conservative boundary. It will keep missing ordinary noun-led continuations. If recall pressure causes repeated noun exceptions, the design becomes another growing word list.
- The 15 pronoun/time exceptions are meaningfully different from the old 799-word vocabulary list. The 24 “discourse markers” are not. `question`, `answer`, `note`, `reminder`, `update`, and `example` are open-class nouns selected from observed data. That is a smaller allowlist, not a closed grammatical class.
- The canary is unnecessary for availability. Apple documents `availableLanguages` as the available spell-checking languages. A fixed nonsense word can itself be learned, producing a false outage. Use `availableLanguages`; treat the canary only as an optional health diagnostic, not the availability authority. See `NSSpellChecker.h:150-160`.

The second carve-out should be removed for round 1. Ship the conservative noun refusal plus the genuinely bounded 15-word exception set, then measure misses without automatically adding exceptions.

### §3 design

PARTIAL.

The dictionary, learned-word refusal, protected-word precedence, conservative tag whitelist, fresh tagger, and case-before-deduplication ordering are structurally sound. Current ordering is proven at `CursorInsertionRepair.swift:341-398`; protected words precede word knowledge at `:474-493`.

Defects:

- The second carve-out is not closed.
- The static canary is not the correct availability authority.
- The synchronous oracle has no deadline. Repair runs directly on the main-actor delivery path at `KernelFinalizationWiring.swift:357-429`. A stalled system service can therefore stall paste, contradicting “the limb cannot damage the heart.”
- The telemetry design is internally inconsistent. §8 promises separate `wordClassUnavailable` and `learnedWord` reasons, but §4 and §10 add only `wordClassNotSafe`.
- `normalizeApostrophes` does have exactly two surviving production consumers outside the deleted type: `CursorInsertionRepair.swift:538-540,724-725`. That helper must survive.
