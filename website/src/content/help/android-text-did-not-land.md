When your words do not appear in the app, EnviousWispr tells you where they went in a short message. Usually you can get them back from the clipboard or from History.

### Read the message

| Message | Where your words are | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Copied. Press and hold, then tap Paste.** | On the clipboard | Press and hold in the text box, then tap **Paste** |
| **Copied too, if it did not arrive.** | On the clipboard, and probably already in the text box | Check the box before you paste |
| **Saved in History. Open EnviousWispr to copy.** | In History | Open the app and copy them |
| **Kept on this phone. Open EnviousWispr to find them.** | On your phone | Open the app and look in History |
| **Your words could not be saved. Please dictate again.** | Not saved | Dictate again |

If the message ends with **Auto-paste is off; turn it back on in Accessibility settings**, EnviousWispr could not paste for you. See [Accessibility Access on Android](/help/android-accessibility-access/).

### Get your words from History

Open EnviousWispr and go to the **History** tab. Find your dictation and tap **Copy**. You see **Copied**, and you can paste the words anywhere.

### Why it happens

**Accessibility access is off.** Without it, EnviousWispr cannot paste into other apps, so it copies your words instead.

**It was a password or PIN field.** EnviousWispr never types into password or PIN fields. The message tells you where your words went.

**You used the Quick Settings tile.** The tile is not tied to a text box, so your words go to the clipboard, or to History if **Auto-copy to clipboard** is off.

### Keep the clipboard as you had it

In Settings, under **Clipboard**, **Auto-copy to clipboard** keeps finished text ready to paste when direct insertion is not possible, and **Restore clipboard after paste** puts back what you had copied before. Both are on unless you turned them off.
