EnviousWispr stores your transcriptions on your Mac, and it keeps a safety copy of your recording so a crash cannot cost you your words. Neither is ever sent to a server, and neither is ever included in our usage analytics. The app does send other information, which is described below and in our privacy policy.

### What Stays On Your Mac

* **Your transcriptions.** Saved to your local transcript history so you can find them again. They live in your user folder and are never uploaded.
* **A safety copy of your recording.** Kept only while a dictation is in progress, then deleted as soon as it finishes. It survives only if the app crashed or was force quit, so your dictation can be recovered on the next launch. It is encrypted on disk.
* **Your custom words, writing styles, and settings.** Yours, on your machine.
* **Your API keys.** Stored on your Mac and sent only to the provider you configured, as part of that request.

### What Is Never Sent Anywhere

* Microphone audio or any sample of it
* Your transcribed text, raw or polished
* The AI polish prompts or the text around your cursor
* Your custom words, writing styles, or transcript history
* Your API keys or tokens
* Your name, email, account, or any personally identifying information
* File paths or your macOS username

### What Is Sent

The app sends anonymous usage and crash diagnostics so we can tell when a release breaks something and which features are worth building on. It is on by default and is not something you turn off.

It contains no dictation content of any kind: no audio, no transcripts, no polished text, no prompts. There is no account, sign-up, or profile it could be attached to. Our privacy policy describes it in full.

If you would rather run without it, EnviousWispr is open source under GPLv3 and you can build it yourself. Dictation works exactly the same either way.

### Cloud AI Polish

If you choose a cloud provider for AI polish, your transcribed text goes straight from your Mac to that provider, under your own API key. There is no Envious Labs server anywhere in that path, so there is nothing for us to see. Audio is never sent to a polish provider, only text. See "AI Polish and Cloud Data" for what each provider receives.
