How search uses markers: a search ignores filler words, and when a search has 3 or more words it may tolerate ONE word that matches nothing. A marker is never ignored and is never that tolerated word. So a marker must be a word whose absence would flip or redirect what the person wants. Over-protecting hurts: a protected word that is also an ordinary setting word makes searches fail.

Rules:
- Aim for at most about 120 words: the most frequent forms people type in short searches (imperative, infinitive, common contractions). Never drop a REQUIRED form to meet that aim.
