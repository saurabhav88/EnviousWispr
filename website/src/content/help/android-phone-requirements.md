EnviousWispr for Android needs Android 13 or newer, a 64-bit ARM phone with at least 6 GB of memory, and about 1.2 GB of free storage for its two on-device models.

### What works best

It works best on recent flagship phones such as the Galaxy S26 Ultra and Pixel 8. Many mid-range phones work too, with dictation and AI polish taking a few seconds longer. On those phones, setup shows **Dictation and on-device AI polish may be slow on this phone.**

### What is not supported

Entry-level phones with older processors are not supported, and neither are Android Go phones. Google Play does not offer the app to most of them.

If the app is installed on a phone it does not support, it shows **This phone isn't supported**, and dictation and model downloads are turned off.

### Storage and setup

The two models take about 1.2 GB: Parakeet Ultra for speech (667.8 MB) and S1-mini for AI polish (484.2 MB). Wi-Fi is recommended for setup. See [Model Downloads and Updates on Android](/help/android-model-downloads/).
