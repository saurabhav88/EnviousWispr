EnviousWispr — Bundled Speaker Diarization Models — Notices and Licence
========================================================================

This app bundles four Core ML models used for offline speaker diarization
(telling different speakers apart in a recording): Segmentation.mlmodelc,
FBank.mlmodelc, Embedding.mlmodelc, and PldaRho.mlmodelc, plus their PLDA
parameters (speaker-plda-parameters.json).

Source and conversion
----------------------
- Source repository: https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/speaker-diarization-coreml
- Source revision: 1ed7a662fdc7109e36d822db793ee6eebdaf8594 (Hugging Face repo commit;
  fetched 2026-09-12 via the Hugging Face Hub API, `lastModified` 2026-03-14). All four
  models and the PLDA parameters JSON are bundled verbatim from this one repository
  revision.
- Distributed by FluidInference as part of the FluidAudio SDK
  (https://github.com/FluidInference/FluidAudio), which is Apache License 2.0.
- The underlying model weights originate from pyannote (segmentation) and
  WeSpeaker (embedding); FluidInference converted them to Core ML format for
  on-device inference. Envious Labs has made no further modification to the
  model weights beyond loading them for inference; the models are unpacked and
  loaded verbatim from the source repository above.
- The parent models (pyannote, WeSpeaker) are licensed CC-BY-4.0, which is why
  this notice and the full licence text below travel with the bundled bytes.

Citations
----------
- Segmentation: Plaquet, A. & Bredin, H. (2023). "Powerset multi-class cross
  entropy loss for neural speaker diarization." Interspeech 2023.
- Embedding: Wang, S. et al. (2023). "Wespeaker: A research and production
  oriented speaker embedding learning toolkit." ICASSP 2023.
- Clustering: Landini, F. et al. (2022). "Bayesian HMM clustering of x-vector
  sequences (VBx) in speaker diarization: theory, implementation and analysis
  on standard tasks."
