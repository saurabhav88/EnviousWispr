Maybe you turned off polishing. You liked the idea of tidy text, yet waiting for it after every dictation felt slow, so you went back to raw transcripts and fixed the filler words by hand.

EnviousWispr 2.6.1 is for you. EG-1, our own polish model, is brand new and now comes in three sizes. It still runs on your Mac, and it is still free.

## What EG-1 does

EG-1 runs after transcription and a first, basic cleanup. It turns what you said into what you meant to write: removing the filler that is left, fixing grammar and punctuation, and sorting out spots where you corrected yourself mid-sentence. Because it runs entirely on your Mac, your words are not sent anywhere.

## Three sizes

Pick a size in **Settings** > **AI Polish**. Each card shows the numbers we measured:

| | EG-1 | EG-1 Mini | EG-1 Micro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best at | Best quality | Fast all-rounder | Smallest and fastest |
| Download | 4.7 GB | 2.1 GB | 805 MB |
| Peak memory in our tests | 5.5 GB | 2.9 GB | 1.6 GB |
| Median polish time, short dictation | 101 ms | 55 ms | 35 ms |
| English score | 97% | 96% | 92% |
| International score | 86% | 82% | 71% |

Each score is the share of answers graded a pass or a minor issue on our own sealed test: more than 4,000 test dictations, graded by an AI judge that does not know which model wrote each answer. "International" covers ten languages: German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese. Polish times are the median across 40 short English dictations on an Apple M5 Max, so expect your own Mac to differ. We timed Mini with a slightly different prompt template from the one that ships.

In plain terms:

- **EG-1** is the most accurate of the three, with the highest scores in the table. Pick it for long dictations, email and languages other than English.
- **EG-1 Mini** stays close to EG-1 on short dictations while using about half the memory.
- **EG-1 Micro** is the lightest. It suits short English dictation on a Mac with little memory to spare.

## It tells you what fits your Mac

You do not have to guess. One card carries a **Best for this Mac** label, based on how much memory your Mac has and the language you dictate in. Above the cards, a live memory bar shows how much memory your Mac is using right now and how much each size would add. If a size would push your Mac past 90% of its memory, the card says so before you download it.

That label is advice, not a lock. You can choose any size, and each one has its own **Download** and **Remove**, so you can try Micro today and switch to the full EG-1 later.

## Already using EG-1?

If you use an eligible older full-size EG-1, you do not need to do anything. Once setup is done, EnviousWispr downloads the new full-size EG-1 on its own. If the upgrade pauses, resume it on the AI Polish page.

## Give polishing another try

If you switched polishing off because it slowed you down, this is a good week to turn it back on. Open **Settings** > **AI Polish**, choose EG-1, and download the size marked for your Mac. Then dictate the way you normally do and see whether the cleaned-up text keeps up.

EG-1 is free, runs on macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon, and needs no account or API key. EnviousWispr itself is free and open source. If you have not tried it yet, you can [download it here](https://enviouswispr.com/).
