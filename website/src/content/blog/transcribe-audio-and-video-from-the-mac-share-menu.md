You recorded a voice memo, a lecture, or a client call, and now you want it as text. Before, that meant opening EnviousWispr and finding the file. In EnviousWispr 2.6.1, you can start from the file itself.

## Share it, then press Start

1. **Turn it on once.** In Finder, open **Share**, choose **Edit Extensions**, and turn on **Transcribe with EnviousWispr**.
2. **Share the file.** Select one supported audio or video file in Finder, Voice Memos, QuickTime or another app that can share a file. Open **Share** and choose **Transcribe with EnviousWispr**.
3. **Check and start.** EnviousWispr opens **Transcribe a File** with your file loaded. Check the speech engine and the polisher, then click **Start transcription**.

When **Saved to History** appears, your transcript is on the **Transcripts** tab of History. Open it there to read, copy or share the text. To listen along, choose **Locate original…** and point to your file.

## Which files work

Share one file at a time in any of these formats: M4A, MP3, WAV, AIFF, CAF, MP4, QuickTime MOV, or FLAC. That covers Voice Memos recordings and many common audio and video files. The file must contain a readable audio track.

## What stays private

EnviousWispr transcribes the audio on your Mac and does not change your original file. If you pick an on-device polisher like EG-1, everything stays on your machine. If you pick a cloud polisher, such as your own OpenAI, Gemini or Claude key or a hosted Ollama model, the transcript, cleanup instructions and any custom words it needs go to that provider. The audio never does.

## Two small things to know

- Finish setting up EnviousWispr first, and the Share option will work.
- If EnviousWispr says an import is already in use, finish or clear it and share again. For any other refusal, follow the message shown.

For everything else about file transcription, from speaker labels to picking an engine, check the [Transcribe a File help page](/help/transcribe-a-file/). EnviousWispr is a free, open-source dictation app for Mac. You can [download it here](https://enviouswispr.com/).
